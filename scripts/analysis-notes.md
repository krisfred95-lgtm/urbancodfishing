Generated by the UCF fish analyser. Size/weight are educated visual estimates from
photos, not measurements. Good-catch rules: **cod (torsk) ≥ 70 cm, any halibut
(kveite), saithe (sei) ≥ 50 cm.**

## Note on the rules
Several **cod are 60–68 cm** — nice fish — but the rule needs **90 cm** for cod, so
they are not flagged "good", while a **52 cm saithe is** (sei needs only 50 cm). If
you'd like, the cod threshold can be lowered (e.g. 70 cm) to highlight the bigger cod.
